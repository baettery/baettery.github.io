ABOUT THE DATA. World Bank World Development Indicators. Nominal GDP = NY.GDP.MKTP.CD; real GDP = NY.GDP.MKTP.KD; annual real growth = NY.GDP.MKTP.KD.ZG; nominal GDP per capita = NY.GDP.PCAP.CD; real GDP per capita at PPP = NY.GDP.PCAP.PP.KD. World GDP share equals national nominal GDP divided by the World Bank WLD aggregate in the same year. Annual GDP ranks reflect reporting economies. ▲▼ rank changes use only economies observed in both comparison years. Exchange rates, inflation, national boundaries, missing observations, and incomplete recent-year reporting affect comparability. GDP per capita does not directly measure income inequality or overall quality of life. Nominal GDP · Growth rate · PPP
